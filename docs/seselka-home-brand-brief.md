# Seselka Home — Brand Brief

> **Doğada köklenir, elde yeniden doğar.** — *Rooted in nature, reborn by hand.*

Handmade home décor **woven by women from recycled paper yarn** ("kağıt ip"), made-to-order in Hatay / İskenderun, Turkey. Quiet-luxury positioning, founded **2026**.

## 3. Origin story & founder

**Sefa Selver** is the founder — described as *"örgünün en yeni öğrencisi"* (weaving's newest student). She found peace (*huzur*) in weaving and turned it into a venture: a way to **support women's labor, produce together, and multiply that calm**. Seselka was born from that search.

Story copy (verbatim themes):
- **"Hayata getiren elden, hayata döndüren ele."** — *From the hand that gives life, to the hand that brings it back to life.*
- *"Here you won't find factory-made products. Each piece is the tangible form of sentences that begin with 'if a woman wills it' — a singular story woven with patience and labor. That's why no two are ever exactly alike."*
- *"Seselka exists to create value — for the women who make, and for those who stand beside them in solidarity. A progressive vision, a mission centered on women's labor, and a promise to share the lived experience along the way."*

> The founder's first woven piece was the **Yuvarlak Sepet** (Round Basket) — "where she learned the stitch, the patience, and the calm." It carries "the first concrete trace of the road she set out on saying 'if a woman wills it.'"

---

## 4. Mission & values

1. **Women's labor / empowerment** — every piece is made by a woman's hand; "making invisible labor visible" (*Görünmeyen emeği, görünür kılmak*). Respect for labor (*Emeğe saygı*).
2. **Sustainability** — recycled paper as the core material; *"Give back what you take from nature, at nature's pace"* (**Doğadan aldığını, doğanın hızında geri ver**). Processed physically (no chemicals), finished in sustainable handmade packaging.
3. **Slow / made-to-order craft** — **no stock, no machines, no molds, no mass production.** Each object is woven only after you order it, uniquely yours.

## 8. Materials & process — "Bir kağıt, dört nefes, bir parça"

*One paper, four breaths, one piece.* Every object passes through the same four stages:

1. **Geri Dönüştürülmüş Kağıt** (Recycled Paper) — starts with ready recycled paper, prepared by hand via physical (not chemical) methods; natural fiber regains value.
2. **Kordon ve Dal Formu** (Cord & Branch Form) — paper cut into thin strips, tightly hand-twisted into organic, hasır-durable paper "branches."
3. **Örgü ve Zanaat** (Weave & Craft) — the cords take form in a woman artisan's hands; patient, calm weaving gives the paper a new, timeless body.
4. **Özen ve İmza** (Care & Signature) — each finished piece is crowned with a **handwritten note + wax seal**, packed in sustainable handmade packaging.

**Care:** dust with a dry soft cloth or brush; keep away from direct water, high humidity, and prolonged sun — so it keeps form and color for years.

## 12. Commerce model

### Pricing
- TRY-denominated; range **₺1,650–8,430** (≈ $35–181).
- **Launch discount: 30%** across the catalog (rounded to nearest ₺10), except items flagged *no-discount* (Hasır Avize).
- "Özel Ölçü" / Özel Seri = **contact for price** ("Konuşalım").

### Order journey ("Sipariş & Ödeme" — 6 steps)
1. **Talep** (Request) — share dream piece, size, color via form (+ inspiration images).
2. **Görüşme & Teklif** (Call & Quote) — feasibility, price, timeline confirmed together.
3. **Görsel Onayı** (Visual Approval) — photos of similar work / drafts shared for sign-off.
4. **Kapora** (Deposit) — **~30% deposit** puts the order into the production queue.
5. **Üretim & İlerleme** (Production & Progress) — periodic progress photos; fully transparent.
6. **Son Onay & Teslim** (Final Approval & Delivery) — final photos, balance collected, carefully packed & shipped.

### Payment
- **30% deposit** to start production; balance before delivery.
- IBAN / hosted card link (Shopier/iyzico): **not yet configured** (placeholders show tasteful "to be shared" copy until live).
- PayPal **not** available to sellers in Türkiye (relevant for any future export).

### Shipping
- **Free, Turkey-wide**, carefully packaged.
- Structured-data signal: handling 14–42 days, transit 1–4 days (≈ the 4–6 week made-to-order window).

### Returns / legal
- Made-to-order / personalized → **exempt from the right of withdrawal** under Turkey's Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği **art. 15/1-b**.
- Defective/damaged arrivals → statutory rights preserved.
- Legal pages live on site: **Mesafeli Satış** (Distance Sales) + **KVKK Aydınlatma** (data/privacy).

## 14. FAQ (positioning answers)

1. **How does made-to-order work?** No stock; six-step process (request → quote → visual approval → deposit → progress updates → packed & shipped).
2. **How much deposit & why?** ~30% on the approved quote, to reserve materials/labor and queue production; balance before delivery.
3. **Lead time?** Varies by piece; made-to-measure typically **4–6 weeks**, confirmed at order.
4. **Ships everywhere in Turkey?** Yes — free, careful packaging.
5. **Returns on custom?** Exempt from withdrawal (15/1-b); defective/damaged rights preserved.
6. **Care?** Dry dusting; avoid water, humidity, prolonged sun.
7. **Really recycled paper?** Yes — recycled paper, hand-twisted into durable organic "branches," then woven.
