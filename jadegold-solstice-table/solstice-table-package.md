# Jade Gold NYC — "Solstice Table" Summer Hero Loop
Production package: scene brief + multi-shot loop shotlist (Seedance 2.0)

## Concept
One sunbeam, one stone table, five moments of light. Gold and crystal jewelry rests on a
sun-baked limestone slab while a single beam of summer sun travels across the scene; only
light, shadow, dust, and one linen edge ever move. The final shot returns to the opening
framing and light angle, closing a seamless loop.

Why this and not the clichés: no beach, no palm shadows, no pool ripples, no ice cubes, no
fruit, no sunglasses. The summer feeling comes from heat-still air, warm stone, and moving
sunlight — a product editorial shot like a still-life painting that happens to breathe.

## Locks (must hold across every shot)
- **Location**: one cream limestone slab table, warm off-white plaster wall behind, nothing else
- **Anchor**: the raw crystal cluster (citrine point + blue calcite + quartz slab) — present or referenced in every shot's geography
- **Light**: one sun, entering from frame left, low and warm; the beam rotates clockwise across the set as the "hours" pass; no second source, no crushed blacks
- **Palette**: warm limestone cream, gold, silver, honey citrine, dusty blue calcite, one green aventurine accent; smooth falloff, no blown highlights on metal
- **Set dressing**: only declared props — crystals, one small brass spoon, one cream ceramic dish, one raw linen cloth edge, one dried fig branch
- **Never**: text, watermark, logo, hands, people, faces, mannequins, reflections of a camera or crew
- **Loop rule**: Shot 5 must match Shot 1's framing and light angle exactly — it is the same table "one day later, same hour"

## Declared defaults (not in your brief — change any of these)
- Aspect ratio: **16:9** hero master (re-crop 4:5/1:1 for feed placements from the master)
- Loop length: **10 s total = 5 shots × 2 s** (Seedance-friendly; alternate cut: 3 shots × 4 s)
- Talent: **tabletop only, no model** — "product editorial videoshooting" read as object-led
- Hero products: all five shared assets appear; Cuban link + evil eye/hamsa carry the most time

## Assembly notes
- Generate Shots 1 and 5 first and compare them as a pair — they are the loop seam; regenerate
  whichever drifts before spending credits on 2–4. Treat them as the course's master/reverse pair.
- Cut on stillness: each shot starts and ends on a settled frame so the joins breathe.
- Attach product reference images (your five approved assets) as Elements per shot:
  S1/S5 → Cuban link + crystals; S2 → rope chains + calcite; S3 → evil eye + hamsa + aventurine;
  S4 → wheat chain + spoon image.
- If a shot slops (oily metal, wobbly chain geometry, invented props), diagnose by the table:
  defect tied to the same feature across reruns → source/reference problem; changes only when the
  motion clause changes → direction problem.
- Sound (optional, off-brand-safe): room tone + distant cicadas at −20 dB. No music = more editorial.
